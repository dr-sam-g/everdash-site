EverDash privacy policy

Last updated: 23.10.2026

Who uses EverDash

EverDash is a private personal app built and used by Sam Gurary. It has exactly one user, its owner. Public sign up is closed. No one else can create an account or connect their data.

What data it handles

Only the owner's own data: his calendar, his email, his files and the notes and tasks he enters. When the owner connects his Google account, EverDash reads Google data he authorizes (such as Calendar events, Gmail messages and Drive files) to show it to him, organize it and prepare drafts for his approval.

How Google data is used

  • Google data is used only to provide EverDash's features to its owner.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.
  • It is stored in a private, encrypted database (Supabase, hosted in Zurich, Switzerland), readable only by the owner's account.
  • To summarize or draft replies, some content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. It is not used to train AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads it.

EverDash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept for as long as the owner uses EverDash and is deleted when he removes it. Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
